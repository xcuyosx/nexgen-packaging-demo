Scope and incorporation

Seller: NexGen Packaging Group. Buyer: The business identified in the accepted quotation or order acknowledgment.

These proposed terms are intended for business sales of packaging products only when expressly incorporated into the parties' agreement. Website browsing, a quote request and its automated receipt do not incorporate these sales terms or create an order.

Artwork and production approval

Buyer must provide accurate specifications and have the rights necessary for Seller and authorized production partners to use supplied artwork for the order. Buyer retains ownership of supplied artwork. Public display or marketing use requires separate permission.

The parties must approve a final specification and any required production proof before custom production begins. Seller must request approval of a material departure from the approved specification. On-screen previews do not replace proof approval.

Prices and payment

The accepted order determines the price and included charges. Any price-adjustment mechanism must be stated and agreed before acceptance. Taxes, exemptions, freight and special handling must be identified in the applicable quotation or acknowledgment.

Credit is available only after separate written approval. Selecting a billing preference or submitting a purchase order does not approve a credit limit or payment period. Any approved credit limit, due date and invoicing instructions will be stated in the written approval or accepted order.
