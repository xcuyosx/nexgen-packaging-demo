Information we receive

You provide your name, company name, email address and account credentials when registering or signing in. Your account may also include a phone number, billing contacts and preferences, receiving addresses, delivery contacts, receiving hours and delivery instructions. Authentication is handled through our authentication provider.

When you request a quote, we receive your selected products, quantities, specifications, print colors, artwork files and placement choices, billing and receiving details, purchase order reference and notes. We keep request references, status information and related communications. We may link these records to your company account and our customer relationship management system. Order information associated with your account may also be displayed.

When you contact us, we receive the contact information and message you submit, including attachments you choose to send through an available channel. Please do not send passwords, full payment card numbers, bank login details, Social Security numbers or other sensitive personal information through quote notes, artwork or the contact form. The storefront does not collect payment at checkout.

Our website and infrastructure providers process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as IP addresses, request times, browser and device information, requested pages and error information. Browser storage and outside services

The storefront uses local browser storage to retain a quote cart, including configuration choices and artwork filenames. The saved cart does not retain the artwork file itself; after a reload, you may need to attach it again. Cart information remains until cleared by the application or your browser.

To prevent duplicate quote submissions after a connection failure, local storage also retains a pending request identifier, reference number and a digest used to recognize the same request. This retry record contains no artwork bytes or contact details and is cleared when the application confirms the submission.

Session storage holds sign-in session information, cached account details and displayed order history. Signing out clears those application records. Browser session behavior, including restoring tabs, depends on your browser. Use sign-out on shared devices rather than relying only on closing a tab.

You can clear or restrict browser storage in your browser settings. Doing so may remove a saved cart or interrupt sign-in. External websites and services have their own privacy practices.
