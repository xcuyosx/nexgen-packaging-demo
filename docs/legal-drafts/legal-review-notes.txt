Research for counsel review

FTC, Protecting Personal Information: A Guide for Business. Inventory information, limit collection and access, set retention rules and plan for incidents. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business

California Attorney General, CCPA. Applicability and rights require business-specific review; business contact information should not be assumed exempt. https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa

California Attorney General, Business Privacy Resources. Review CalOPPA disclosures and online tracking requirements where relevant. https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/business-privacy

FTC, CAN-SPAM Act Compliance Guide. Assess messages by their primary purpose; an inquiry acknowledgment should not be assumed exempt merely because it is called transactional. Review final content before sending. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

Missouri Revised Statutes 400.2-204. Illustrates that a sales contract can arise through conduct as well as a formal writing; Missouri applicability is unconfirmed. https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=400.2-204

CFPB, Regulation B 1002.9. Counsel should determine applicable business-credit notification procedures before using the credit application. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/9/
